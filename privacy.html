Privacy Policy – Sonnenberg Sync

Sonnenberg Sync is a private tool used only by its owner (Willy Sonnenberg).

It accesses Google Drive solely to create and update the owner's own Project Sonnenberg files (data, configuration, logs, reports) in the owner's Drive folder. It does not read, collect or share any other data, does not use data for advertising, and does not transfer data to third parties.

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
